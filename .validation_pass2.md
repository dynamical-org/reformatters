### A3 — Five instantaneous variables come from the previous forecast cycle at 00/06/12/18 UTC

`src/reformatters/noaa/gfs/analysis/region_job.py:47-53`:

```python
group_has_hour_0 = item({var.has_hour_0_values() for var in data_var_group})
```

At a synoptic hour T the two groups diverge: the hour-0 group gets `init=T, lead=0` (the analysis), the not-hour-0 group gets `init=T-6h, lead=6` (the previous cycle's 6-hour forecast). At all 20 other hours both groups land on the same init and lead.

# PART B — User-facing findings

Only what a reader of the published dataset can act on, in the three categories the dataset owner allows. Findings that fit none of them (A3, A4, A5, A7, A8, A9, A10) are in Part A only; A2's durable explanation belongs in the variables' `comment`, and appears here under the masking exception in docs/validation.md §3e.

### Unavailable data

- **Correction to the currently published note on the potential-vorticity variables.** The 48 variables on the ±0.5, ±1, ±1.5 and ±2 PVU surfaces are not absent in an equatorial band. Each is absent over the *opposite* hemisphere: the positive-PVU variables have no data across the southern extratropics (93–100% absent south of 30°S) and are complete north of 10°N; the negative-PVU variables are the mirror. The deep tropics are 95–97% present in both families.

### Variables to mask

- **The eight soil-moisture variables read 1.0 over permanent ice, which is a placeholder rather than a measurement — mask values at or above 0.9.** This covers Antarctica, the Greenland ice sheet and the Arctic ice caps: about 34% of all land cells, or 11% of the grid. The value does not change through the year, and no real soil cell anywhere in the archive reaches 0.9. The affected variables are the four `volumetric_soil_moisture_*` and the four `liquid_volumetric_soil_moisture_*`. Left unmasked, they pull any land average sharply toward saturation and flatten the colour range of any map.

### Source data changes over time

- **From 2022-02-28 18:00 UTC, `liquid_volumetric_soil_moisture_100_200cm` is reported to only two decimal places instead of three, and can exceed the total soil moisture at the same depth.** The source began publishing this one field on a 0.01 grid — 98 distinct values worldwide instead of about 850. Nothing else changed: the other seven soil-moisture variables, including this one's own total counterpart, kept full precision. Because the two fields are now rounded differently, `liquid_volumetric_soil_moisture_100_200cm` exceeds `volumetric_soil_moisture_100_200cm` at 20–57% of land cells, by up to 0.010 (typically 0.0004). Anyone deriving frozen soil water as total minus liquid at 100–200 cm will get small negative values from this date on. The coarser precision continues past the end of this archive window and is present until 2022-07-03 06:00 UTC.
