# Review notes — noaa-gfs-analysis-virtual (merged, both passes)

Window validated: 2021-05-01 to 2022-05-01. Not exhaustive — see limitations at the end.

#### Unavailable data

- **Nine hourly positions on 2022-11-29 and 2022-11-30 are missing one or both source
  products.** Empty for the pgrb2 variable set at 20:00 on the 29th and at 02:00, 05:00,
  09:00, 10:00 and 11:00 on the 30th; empty for the pgrb2b set at 22:00 on the 29th and at
  09:00 and 11:00 on the 30th. Three further positions — 21:00 and 08:00 for pgrb2, 02:00
  on the 30th for pgrb2b — hold references that fail to decode. The source files exist
  upstream; these are gaps in this archive, not a source outage.
- **Land-surface fields are empty over water.** The four volumetric and four liquid
  volumetric soil moisture variables, field capacity, wilting point, vegetation, plant
  canopy surface water and water runoff follow the land-sea mask exactly.
- **Snow, soil temperature, ground heat flux and potential evaporation are empty over open
  water but present over land and sea ice**, so their coverage boundary moves with the
  sea-ice edge through the year.
- **ice_temperature_surface is empty wherever there is no ice**, about 87% of the grid.
- **Cloud base, top and cloud-top temperature fields are empty where the matching cloud
  cover is zero.** The six high, middle and low pressure fields and three cloud-top
  temperatures are empty over 18-41% of the grid. The two convective fields are empty over
  75-78%, and there the source withholds a value even where convective cover is nonzero.
- **Potential-vorticity-surface fields are absent where that signed surface does not occur
  in the column, which is hemispheric rather than tropical.** Positive-PVU surfaces are
  largely absent in the southern extratropics and negative-PVU surfaces in the northern,
  mirroring each other; the deep tropics are mostly present. The absent fraction is 33-40%
  of the grid globally and varies with threshold and season. Covers geopotential height,
  pressure, temperature, u and v wind and vertical speed shear on each of the eight
  surfaces.
- **In the height-above-mean-sea-level group, values are absent where terrain rises above
  the level** — 22.8% of the grid at 305 m, falling to 0.2% at 4,572 m.
- **Eight pressure-level variables carry no data over part of the level set at every step.**
  cloud_cover, cloud_mixing_ratio, graupel, ice_water_mixing_ratio, rain_mixing_ratio and
  snow_mixing_ratio are empty from 40 down to 0.01 hPa; specific_humidity and
  ozone_mixing_ratio are empty at the sixteen levels from 125 to 875 hPa.
- **haines_index_surface is empty over about 58-60% of the grid, chiefly ocean.**

#### Values to mask

- **percent_frozen_precipitation_surface — mask values below -0.1.** Negative values mark
  no precipitation, span -50 to -0.1, and cover 82-84% of the grid.
- **geopotential_height_cloud_ceiling — mask values above 19,000 m.** Those mark no cloud
  ceiling, span 19,000 to 20,000 m, and cover 46-52% of the grid.
- **albedo_surface — exclude zeros before averaging.** The value is exactly 0 wherever the
  averaging window received no sunlight; a mean over all hours is not an albedo.

#### Source data changes over time

- **Deep-soil liquid moisture becomes ten times coarser on 2022-02-28T18:00.**
  liquid_volumetric_soil_moisture_100_200cm moves from a 0.001 to a 0.010 value grid and
  stays there until 2022-07-03T06:00. Its total counterpart is unchanged, so liquid exceeds
  total at 20-57% of land cells by up to 0.0104 in that window; deriving frozen soil water
  as total minus liquid yields small negative values there.

#### Limitations of this validation

Vertical-group variables were reviewed at one level each. Source fidelity was checked
element-wise on a sample of arrays and positions, not archive-wide. Availability was
verified exhaustively across the window; drift, sentinels and value distributions were
swept across all variables but at sampled positions.
