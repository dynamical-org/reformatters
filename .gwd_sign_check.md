# GFS U-GWD / V-GWD sign check

## Verdict

**The existing CF standard names are backwards for the values GFS publishes as
U-GWD and V-GWD. Confidence: high.**

NCEP's diagnostic has the sign of the surface-side reaction stress. In the
physics source, each positive eastward/northward atmospheric wind tendency is
accumulated into `DUSFC`/`DVSFC` with a minus sign. Positive U-GWD therefore
corresponds to a westward gravity-wave-drag force on the atmosphere (and an
eastward reaction on the surface); positive V-GWD likewise corresponds to a
southward force on the atmosphere. CF's names have the opposite contract:
positive values tend to accelerate the atmosphere eastward/northward.

This conclusion comes from the defining source equations and the direct output
mapping, not from observed field statistics. No metadata was changed.

## 1. NCEP definition and sign

### 1a. The GFS GWD scheme defines a surface stress

The scheme describes its two stages as computing surface stress and then
distributing it through the column:

- `physics/GWD/gwdps.f`,
  [lines 27-30](https://github.com/NCAR/ccpp-physics/blob/3e6660c6df54e95a0871e990c2294dd397ae3860/physics/GWD/gwdps.f#L27-L30): the scheme computes surface stress and distributes it over the vertical column.
- Same file,
  [lines 63-68](https://github.com/NCAR/ccpp-physics/blob/3e6660c6df54e95a0871e990c2294dd397ae3860/physics/GWD/gwdps.f#L63-L68): it calls this the stress "induced from the surface."
- `physics/GWD/gwdps.meta`,
  [lines 222-237](https://github.com/NCAR/ccpp-physics/blob/3e6660c6df54e95a0871e990c2294dd397ae3860/physics/GWD/gwdps.meta#L222-L237), defines `dusfc` and `dvsfc` as the zonal and meridional surface stress due to orographic GWD, in Pa.

### 1b. Its sign is the negative of the atmospheric momentum tendency

The clearest defining equations are in `physics/GWD/drag_suite.F90`:

- [Lines 1137-1146](https://github.com/NCAR/ccpp-physics/blob/3e6660c6df54e95a0871e990c2294dd397ae3860/physics/GWD/drag_suite.F90#L1137-L1146)
  form the x/y GWD components and add them to `dudt`/`dvdt`.
- [Lines 1171-1174](https://github.com/NCAR/ccpp-physics/blob/3e6660c6df54e95a0871e990c2294dd397ae3860/physics/GWD/drag_suite.F90#L1171-L1174)
  subtract the same components, pressure-weighted, into `dusfc`/`dvsfc`:

  ```fortran
  dudt(i,k) = tmp1 + dudt(i,k)
  dvdt(i,k) = tmp2 + dvdt(i,k)
  ...
  dusfc(i) = dusfc(i) - onebgrcs * (...x tendency...) * del(i,k)
  dvsfc(i) = dvsfc(i) - onebgrcs * (...y tendency...) * del(i,k)
  ```

The original GFS `gwdps` implementation makes the same relation especially
compact:

- `physics/GWD/gwdps.f`,
  [line 445](https://github.com/NCAR/ccpp-physics/blob/3e6660c6df54e95a0871e990c2294dd397ae3860/physics/GWD/gwdps.f#L445), sets `onebg = 1.0 / g`.
- `physics/GWD/gwdps.meta`,
  [lines 103-106](https://github.com/NCAR/ccpp-physics/blob/3e6660c6df54e95a0871e990c2294dd397ae3860/physics/GWD/gwdps.meta#L103-L106), defines `DEL` as a pressure difference in Pa.
- `physics/GWD/gwdps.f`,
  [lines 1264-1272](https://github.com/NCAR/ccpp-physics/blob/3e6660c6df54e95a0871e990c2294dd397ae3860/physics/GWD/gwdps.f#L1264-L1272), applies `DTAUX`/`DTAUY` to the wind while accumulating the opposite sign into the surface stress:

  ```fortran
  B(J,K)   = DTAUX
  ...
  DUSFC(J) = DUSFC(J) - onebg * DTAUX * DEL(J,K)
  DVSFC(J) = DVSFC(J) - onebg * DTAUY * DEL(J,K)
  ```

Thus, apart from the positive mass weighting `DEL/g`, the diagnostic is

```text
DUSFC = - sum_k[(du/dt)_GWD * DEL_k/g]
DVSFC = - sum_k[(dv/dt)_GWD * DEL_k/g].
```

This is a direct sign definition. It makes positive `DUSFC`/`DVSFC` the
surface-side reaction to a westward/southward atmospheric GWD tendency, not a
stress tending to accelerate the atmosphere eastward/northward.

The alternative/legacy GWD driver independently confirms the convention:
`physics/GWD/ugwp_driver_v0.F90`,
[lines 875-901](https://github.com/NCAR/ccpp-physics/blob/3e6660c6df54e95a0871e990c2294dd397ae3860/physics/GWD/ugwp_driver_v0.F90#L875-L901), adds `DTAUX`/`DTAUY` to atmospheric tendencies, accumulates them, and finally multiplies the surface diagnostics by `-1/g`.

### 1c. The sign is preserved all the way to U-GWD/V-GWD

There is no intervening sign reversal:

1. `physics/Interstitials/UFS_SCM_NEPTUNE/GFS_GWD_generic_post.F90`,
   [lines 103-105](https://github.com/NCAR/ccpp-physics/blob/3e6660c6df54e95a0871e990c2294dd397ae3860/physics/Interstitials/UFS_SCM_NEPTUNE/GFS_GWD_generic_post.F90#L103-L105), time-integrates `dusfcg`/`dvsfcg` directly into `dugwd`/`dvgwd`.
2. `ccpp/driver/GFS_diagnostics.F90`,
   [lines 1237-1255](https://github.com/NOAA-EMC/ufsatm/blob/f3b88bdfaa092ba30ca0afb5f7969f5d95324fb7/ccpp/driver/GFS_diagnostics.F90#L1237-L1255), exposes those exact arrays as time-averaged `u-gwd_ave` and `v-gwd_ave`, with conversion factor one.
3. `sorc/ncep_post.fd/INITPOST_NETCDF.f`,
   [lines 3009-3018](https://github.com/NOAA-EMC/UPP/blob/fe4f02b41fd789e414fb4bf23ad27c58ef058ee4/sorc/ncep_post.fd/INITPOST_NETCDF.f#L3009-L3018), reads `u-gwd_ave`/`v-gwd_ave` directly into `gtaux`/`gtauy`.
4. `sorc/ncep_post.fd/SURFCE.f`,
   [lines 6482-6519](https://github.com/NOAA-EMC/UPP/blob/fe4f02b41fd789e414fb4bf23ad27c58ef058ee4/sorc/ncep_post.fd/SURFCE.f#L6482-L6519) and
   [6523-6557](https://github.com/NOAA-EMC/UPP/blob/fe4f02b41fd789e414fb4bf23ad27c58ef058ee4/sorc/ncep_post.fd/SURFCE.f#L6523-L6557), copies `GTAUX`/`GTAUY` directly into the encoded output grids.
5. `parm/post_avblflds.xml`,
   [lines 2719-2736](https://github.com/NOAA-EMC/UPP/blob/fe4f02b41fd789e414fb4bf23ad27c58ef058ee4/parm/post_avblflds.xml#L2719-L2736), maps those output fields to GRIB parameter names `U-GWD` and `V-GWD` at the surface.

### 1d. Official NCEP GRIB2 table

Official source: [NCEP GRIB2 Table 4.2-0-3, Meteorological products, Mass category](https://www.nco.ncep.noaa.gov/pmb/docs/grib2/grib2_doc/grib2_table4-2-0-3.shtml).

Exact rows in that section:

- Parameter 16: "Zonal Flux of Gravity Wave Stress", `N m-2`, `U-GWD`.
- Parameter 17: "Meridional Flux of Gravity Wave Stress", `N m-2`, `V-GWD`.
- Local-use parameters 194 and 195 repeat the same two definitions.

The GRIB table identifies the quantity, components, and units, but supplies no
positive-direction or force-recipient note. It is therefore not independently
decisive about sign; the producing equations above are decisive.

## 2. CF definitions

Official source: CF Standard Name Table 94,
`Data/cf-standard-names/current/src/cf-standard-name-table.xml`.

- `atmosphere_eastward_stress_due_to_gravity_wave_drag`,
  [lines 401-404](https://github.com/cf-convention/cf-convention.github.io/blob/7ca6c3562003b7b851d16ffa10e672f2f3126058/Data/cf-standard-names/current/src/cf-standard-name-table.xml#L401-L404), canonical units Pa. Its direction clause is
  "positive when directed eastward (negative westward)"; the remainder says this stress tends to accelerate the atmosphere in the named direction.
- `atmosphere_northward_stress_due_to_gravity_wave_drag`,
  [lines 1616-1619](https://github.com/cf-convention/cf-convention.github.io/blob/7ca6c3562003b7b851d16ffa10e672f2f3126058/Data/cf-standard-names/current/src/cf-standard-name-table.xml#L1616-L1619), canonical units Pa. Its direction clause is
  "positive when directed northward (negative southward)" and it has the same atmosphere-acceleration rule.

The important CF recipient is explicitly the atmosphere. Therefore a positive
CF eastward stress means eastward atmospheric acceleration, and a positive CF
northward stress means northward atmospheric acceleration.

## 3. Comparison and answer

| Positive published value | NCEP producing code | Current CF name asserts |
|---|---|---|
| U-GWD | Westward GWD force on atmosphere; eastward reaction stress at surface | Eastward force/acceleration of atmosphere |
| V-GWD | Southward GWD force on atmosphere; northward reaction stress at surface | Northward force/acceleration of atmosphere |

Accordingly, applying
`atmosphere_eastward_stress_due_to_gravity_wave_drag` and
`atmosphere_northward_stress_due_to_gravity_wave_drag` directly to the
unmodified GFS U-GWD/V-GWD values is not justified. Both names are backwards
relative to the values' source-defined sign. Keeping those CF names would
require negating the GFS fields; keeping the values unchanged requires metadata
that does not claim the CF atmosphere-acceleration convention.

The evidence establishes the sign mismatch. It does not by itself authorize a
breaking metadata or value change to the published archive.
