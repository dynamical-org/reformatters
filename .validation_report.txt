  Variables to mask

  - percent_frozen_precipitation_surface — mask values below −0.1. Negative values mark no precipitation and span −50 to −0.1; they cover 82–84% of the
    grid.
  - geopotential_height_cloud_ceiling — mask values above 19,000 m. Values there mark no cloud ceiling and span 19,000 to 20,000 m; they cover 46–52% of
    the grid.
  - albedo_surface — mask zeros before averaging. The value is exactly 0 wherever the averaging window received no sunlight, about 32% of the grid at any
    time; a mean taken over all hours is not an albedo.

  Source data changes over time

  - sunshine_duration_surface covers a shorter period at 00, 06, 12 and 18 UTC than at other hours. At those four hours the value is a 3-hour total,
    reaching at most 10,800 s. At the other twenty hours it is a 1- to 5-hour total since the preceding 00, 06, 12 or 18 UTC hour, reaching at most 18,000
    s at the fifth hour. The series therefore steps down at each of those four hours instead of continuing to accumulate, and no value in the archive
    reaches 21,600 s.
